Four questions that matter more than the EMI

Answer these honestly before comparing any lease quote.

1. How long will you keep the vehicle?

Short horizon (2–4 years) often favours leasing. Long horizon usually favours buying.

2. Who carries residual value risk?

EVs can depreciate quickly. Leasing shifts that risk — and you pay for the shift.

3. What is your real annual mileage?

High km makes the lower energy cost of EVs more valuable. Low km reduces the edge.

4. Can you charge at home or office?

Home charging is a major practical and cost advantage. Public-only charging changes the math.
